1 · Define the problem

The forum lives in five places, and none of them guard what a newcomer can see

Problem statement: the seven members of the Shadow Forum have no single place for their forum. Agendas sit in two Lark wiki spaces, dates sit in a group chat, the contest runs on screenshots, and the website is a landing page with a login. As two new members join on 23 December, the forum cannot show them the present without also showing them a past they were not in the room for.

Who feels it

  • Founding members (four, since the 19 Jan 2026 kickoff). Their personal and business disclosures are in the agendas. A leak to a newcomer breaks the trust the forum runs on, and it cannot be undone.
  • Nam Trung, in the forum since 28 Jul 2026, and Minh Anh, from 23 Dec 2026, and Daniel, from the January 2027 forum. They should start clean and still have everything from their own time in one place.
  • Contest riders. WHOOP scores are shared as screenshots, the race is tallied by hand, and goal progress is not tracked between coaching calls.
  • Dave as moderator and admin. He keeps the dates, the access, the kitty and the reminders in his head and in chat.

What it costs

  • Trust. One pre-join agenda reaching a newcomer, through a page, the chatbot, a group email or Lark itself, is enough to damage the forum.
  • Missed and double-booked forums, because the date is buried in chat.
  • A contest that is hard to believe in. Up to 19M VND changes hands on 31 Dec, and today the numbers behind it are screenshots.
  • Dave's time, nagging, tallying and copying dates by hand every month.

Why now

  • 23 Dec 2026: Minh Anh's first forum (Daniel's is in January). The access rule must be live, and tested, before they first sign in.
  • 31 Dec 2026: the Q4 contest ends and the kitty is settled. The race and the settlement need real data by then.
  • The assistant and an MCP connection are planned. Building the access rule in first is far cheaper than retrofitting it.
  • The current schema (0001_init.sql) lets every member read every goal, WHOOP day and penalty, and lets members edit every column of their own profile. Both must change before anyone new joins.

Where content can leak

Lark wiki spaces

Access is per space, not per date. Adding the December members to "2026/7 Forum" would show them Jul–Nov.

Website and app

Built naively ("any member reads"), every member sees everything, including old Q4 goals.

Assistant

With the admin (service-role) key or a shared index, it ignores every per-member rule.

MCP connection

A shared key means any connected client sees everything.

Group emails, push and agents

A monthly report or a lock-screen preview that quotes a pre-December forum reaches the newcomers.

Local exports

Dave's Lark export can see all 16 pages. Committed to git or loaded into a tool, it bypasses every rule.

iPhone app v2 (28 Nov)

The app built on 27 Nov covers the first plan, but the website has moved on: the Kitty ledger, Constitution v2 with e-sign, photos, Topics (the Parking Lot) and recaps, Biometrics with journal insights, Ask AI conversations and the Claude connector. Members who open the app would find half the forum missing, and a newcomer could be sent to a website screen the app can't open. Every new screen is also a new place a pre-join item could leak.

The rule, written once

-- active member who joined on or before this date
create function public.can_see_date(d date) returns boolean
language sql stable security definer set search_path = public as $$
  select exists (select 1 from profiles
    where id = auth.uid() and forum_left_on is null and d >= forum_joined_on);
$$;

-- a season is visible only if it started on or after you joined
create function public.can_see_season(s uuid) returns boolean
language sql stable security definer set search_path = public as $$
  select exists (select 1 from seasons where id = s and can_see_date(starts_on));
$$;

create policy "meetings from join date"  on meetings       for select to authenticated using (can_see_date(held_on));
create policy "chunks from join date"    on meeting_chunks for select to authenticated using (can_see_date(held_on));
create policy "coaching from join date"  on coaching_notes for select to authenticated
  using (can_see_date(created_at::date) and (author_id = auth.uid() or rider_id = auth.uid() or coach_of(rider_id)));
create policy "goals in visible seasons" on goals          for select to authenticated using (can_see_season(season_id));
-- same season policy on whoop_days, goal_progress, penalties; library_docs by audience

revoke update on profiles from authenticated;
grant  update (full_name, avatar_url) on profiles to authenticated;

No date, no rows. A member who has left sees nothing. Writes to forum tables are admin or server only.

3 · Design the workflow

Every door goes through the same gate

Nothing that answers a member, whether a page, the app, the assistant or an MCP tool, holds a key stronger than that member's own login.

WebsiteServer components read with the member's session.
iPhone appsupabase-js with the member's session in expo-secure-store.
Ask/api/ask searches through an RPC that runs as the member.
MCPEach member connects with their own sign-in. Tools run as them.
Agents, email, pushRead only what every recipient can see.
Postgres row-level security: can_see_date() and can_see_season() for the signed-in member. If a door forgets, the gate still says no.

Repo layout

  • The Next.js app moves into website/; the Expo app goes in mobile/; shared types (database.types.ts), season maths and the kitty rules go in shared/.
  • Vercel's root directory becomes website/, with an ignored-build step so changes only under mobile/ do not rebuild the site.
  • Universal Links: website/public/.well-known/apple-app-site-association.

Workflow A · A new member joins

  • Dave opens Me → Members and access, adds the person with their email and first forum date (23 Dec for Minh Anh, the January forum for Daniel), and sends the invite.
  • Before sending, he taps Preview as member and checks the Forum list starts at their first forum and the Contest tab shows no Q4 season.
  • In Lark, outside the app, they are added only to a new "2026/12 Forum" space. This step is on the checklist in the same screen.

Workflow B · Forum dates and agendas

  • Dave sets the month's date once, in EO Vietnam. That night it appears on Home and in Forum as a planned forum, and members get a push: "Next forum: Wed 23 Dec, 6 pm".
  • After the forum, the agenda is written in Lark as today. Dave runs the import (Phase 2: a local script; later, a nightly job). The page is matched by date, and the meeting becomes "held".
  • The push says only "A new agenda is in Forum". Lock-screen text never shows agenda content.

Workflow C · WHOOP sync

  • A rider taps Connect WHOOP in Me (or on Contest). WHOOP's sign-in opens, they approve read access to recovery, cycles and sleep, and the app backfills from the season's first day.
  • WHOOP webhooks plus a nightly sweep keep whoop_days current. A missed day still scores as the lowest recorded score, by the contest rules.
  • The CSV upload stays as a fallback for anyone who will not connect.

Workflow D · The contest month

  • Steward (daily) recomputes standings. Whip (07:00 daily) nudges anyone with missing data, by push and Lark.
  • Riders update goal progress, status and a note for their coach before the monthly 1-on-1. Coaching notes are seen by the rider, the coach and admins.
  • Chronicler (last day of the month) writes the report. Bookkeeper keeps the kitty and produces the 31 Dec settlement.
  • Every agent output that goes to a group only uses seasons and meetings visible to its latest-joined recipient. The December members are therefore left out of Q4 reports, rather than reports being watered down for everyone.

Workflow E · Ask and MCP

  • Ask runs on the member's session. Search goes through match_meeting_chunks(), declared security invoker, so the database filters before the model sees anything. Answers cite their meetings.
  • With no match, the assistant says it has no record. It never says "that was before you joined", because that would confirm the content exists.
  • MCP (/api/mcp) offers read-only tools: list_meetings, get_agenda, search_agendas, my_contest. Each member connects with their own OAuth sign-in from Me. There is no shared key.
  • A lint rule bans importing the service-role key in website/ask, website/mcp and mobile/.

Decisions behind the map

  • Forum holds everything about the forum itself. Agenda, Topics, Photos, Kitty and Resources are its sub-tabs, in that order. Photos and kitty lines are each tagged to a forum.
  • The Kitty moves from Contest to Forum. The website's Kitty is the forum's own ledger, which runs all year. The contest's money page becomes Penalties, so the two can't be confused.
  • Goals move into Contest. They're set per season, like the race. Off season, the Contest tab opens on Goals, so it's never empty.
  • Biometrics goes under Me. It's your own data and private to you, and the race already shows everyone's season scores.
  • Ask AI floats bottom right, above the tab bar, on every screen except Ask itself and the Constitution gate, as on the website. Opening it from a forum recap scopes the question to that forum.
  • Sub-tabs follow Neon Noir: uppercase text with a red underline, never boxed buttons. The app's current tokens in mobile/src/theme/tokens.ts still draw cards and rounded buttons. They move to the website's :root values (no boxes, 2px buttons, Archivo condensed titles) in the first app PR.
  • Routes stay the website's paths. /gallery, /kitty, /goals, /biometrics and /chat are added as app routes too, so every Universal Link and push still opens the right screen.
The rule on every screen. The app never filters by date, season or audience. It asks Supabase on the member's own session, and row-level security (can_see_date(), can_see_season(), the library audience) decides what comes back. A screen that gets nothing back shows its empty state, never a lock or a count. Leak tests for the four personas (founding, Nam Trung, a December member, a leaver) run against each new query before it merges.

Trust you can prove, and a forum that runs itself

FAST goal
By 23 Dec 2026, all seven members can open the new site and see their next forum and their own agendas, with zero pre-join content reachable by any channel, proven by tests. By 28 Feb 2027, all seven use the iPhone app every month.

The return

  • Members keep sharing openly, because the forum can show exactly what a newcomer can and cannot see.
  • The assistant and MCP are safe because of how they are built, not because of careful prompting.
  • Every future intake costs one date field, not an access audit.
  • Dave stops nagging, tallying and copying dates. The Whip, the Steward and EO Vietnam do it.
  • The contest's money moves on numbers everyone trusts.

iPhone app v2: success

  • Coverage table: every website feature is either in the app or marked web-only with a reason. 0 rows left as "update" or "new" by the end of the build.
  • Leak tests for the four personas cover every new app query (photos, kitty, topics, recap, constitution, chat, Ask).
  • A kitty line or a photo can be added from the phone in under 30 seconds.
5 · Definition of Done

Finished means

  • Phases 1–5 merged to main through PRs and deployed by git push → Vercel at eoshadowforum.com; the iPhone app built by EAS from GitHub, approved by App Review and installed by all seven members from its unlisted App Store link.
  • Leak tests run in CI and block merges.
  • All seven members have a join date; Minh Anh's and Daniel's are set before their invites go out.
  • Dave has used Preview as member for Nam Trung, Minh Anh and Daniel.
  • The "2026/12 Forum" Lark space exists, and the December members are in it and in no older space.
  • No Lark export is in git, and no forum content sits in a shared tool outside this model.
  • The Q4 settlement is produced from synced data on 31 Dec.
  • Members get one short note: what the app and the assistant can see, that both start from each member's own first forum, how to connect WHOOP, and who to ask about access.

Out of scope for now: Android, recording coaching calls in the app (the shared recorder module from Forever could be added later), editing agendas in the app (Lark stays the place to write them).
