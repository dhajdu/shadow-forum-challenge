The machinery

The Agents

Four scheduled agents run the contest so no human has to nag, tally, or write it up. They pull the data, keep everyone honest, and turn the standings into a monthly "horse race."

The WhipDaily
Accountability

The Whip — keeps everyone honest

Checks each rider wore the band and logged a score yesterday. Missing data or a skipped day triggers a friendly (then less friendly) nudge in the group chat, and flags it as the day's lowest score per the rules. Weekly, it posts a "who's slipping" summary.

Trigger: daily 07:00→ Lark / email nudge
The StewardDaily
Scoring

The Steward — runs Race Into the Shadow

Pulls every rider's WHOOP scores, computes running averages, ranks the field, and updates the live standings + provisional payout ladder. Owns the Race Into the Shadow board — each rider a silhouette moving down a neon lane from the light into the shadow as their average shifts; deepest in leads.

Trigger: daily→ Standings + ladder
The ChroniclerMonthly
Reporting

The Chronicler — writes the monthly report

End of each month, compiles standings movement (who moved up/down), biggest mover and faller, a per-rider recap, business-goal status, and coaching-call highlights into one report emailed to the group. Pulls summaries from the AI Coaching Assistant where riders opted in.

Trigger: last day of month→ Emailed report
The BookkeeperMonthly + EOQ
The kitty

The Bookkeeper — tallies the kitty

Tracks what each rider owes: contest placement (2M–5M) plus any missed business goal (+5M rider, +5M coach). Keeps a running kitty total, and at quarter-end produces the final settlement — who pays whom, and where the pot goes.

Trigger: monthly + Dec 31→ Settlement sheet

Data flow: WHOOP → the Whip (completeness) → the Steward (scoring & race) → the Chronicler (report) → the Bookkeeper (kitty). Standings live on the site; reports land in the group's inbox each month.
